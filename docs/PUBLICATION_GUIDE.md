# Research Publication & Reproducibility Guide

A guide for computational and synthetic organic chemists submitting manuscripts featuring NLE-Predictor modeling results.

---

## 1. What Information to Report in Papers

When reporting predicted NLE curves or experimental parameter estimations in journal articles (*JACS*, *Angewandte Chemie*, *ACS Catalysis*, *Chemical Science*, *J. Chem. Inf. Model.*), include the following standard details in the **Computational Details** or **Supporting Information**:

### 1.1 Quantum Chemistry Settings
- **Conformational Sampling:** State CREST and xTB versions, energy window (e.g., $6.0\text{ kcal/mol}$), and ALPB solvent model.
- **DFT Electronic Structure:** Functional (e.g., B3LYP, $\omega\text{B97X-D3}$), basis set (e.g., def2-SVP, def2-TZVP), dispersion model (D4 / D3BJ), and continuum solvent model (CPCM / SMD).
- **Ensemble Weighting:** Report the number of conformers included per dimer and verify that all evaluated structures exhibited zero imaginary vibrational frequencies ($N_{\text{imag}} = 0$).
- **Standard-State Corrections:** State whether gas-phase free energies were adjusted by the standard-state concentration shift ($\Delta G^\circ_{\text{corr}} = +1.894\text{ kcal/mol}$ at 298.15 K).

### 1.2 Kinetic & Thermodynamic Parameters
- **Gibbs Free Energies:** Report $G(RR)$, $G(RS)$, and the relative stability $\Delta G = G(RS) - G(RR)$ in $\text{kcal/mol}$.
- **Association Constants:** Report assumed or fitted $K_{\text{homo}}$ and the resulting ratio $K_{\text{hetero}} / K_{\text{homo}} = 2 \exp(-\Delta G / RT)$.
- **Regression Statistics:** When fitting experimental data, report:
  - Estimated $\Delta G$ with standard error: e.g., $\Delta G = -1.74 \pm 0.12\text{ kcal/mol}$ (95% CI: $[-1.98, -1.50]$)
  - Coefficient of determination ($R^2$) and Root Mean Square Error (RMSE).

## 2. Standard Computational Methods Paragraph Template

You may adapt the following text for your manuscript's *Computational Methods* section:

> "Computational modeling of catalyst dimerization and non-linear effects was performed using the NLE-Predictor computational framework (v1.0.0).\cite{raza_nle_predictor_2026} Conformational ensembles of homochiral $(R,R)$ and heterochiral $(R,S)$ catalyst dimers were sampled using CREST (v2.12) coupled with GFN2-xTB within a $6.0\text{ kcal/mol}$ energetic window.\cite{Grimme2017CREST} The lowest-energy conformers were refined with density functional theory in ORCA (v5.0) at the B3LYP/def2-SVP level with Grimme D4 dispersion and CPCM solvation.\cite{Neese2020ORCA} Harmonic vibrational frequencies were calculated at the same level of theory to ensure all stationary points corresponded to local minima ($N_{\text{imag}} = 0$) and to obtain thermal corrections to the Gibbs free energy. Final free energies were Boltzmann-weighted at $298.15\text{ K}$. The resulting free energy difference $\Delta G = G(RS) - G(RR)$ was propagated through Kagan's $\text{ML}_2$ reservoir mass-balance model using an analytical Jacobian solver to predict product enantiomeric excess ($ee_{\text{prod}}$) across catalyst optical purity ($ee_{\text{cat}}$)."

## 3. Recommended Figure Formatting

For submission to ACS and RSC journals:
- **Resolution:** 600 DPI for PNG images or vector formats (PDF, SVG).
- **Figure Dimensions:** Single-column width: 8.5 cm (3.35 in); double-column width: 17.5 cm (6.9 in).
- **Typography:** Arial or Helvetica sans-serif fonts, 10–12 pt labels, bold titles.
- **Color Palettes:** Colorblind-friendly palettes (ColorBrewer Dark2 or Viridis).

All figures generated by `scripts/generate_paper_figures.py` automatically conform to these specifications.
