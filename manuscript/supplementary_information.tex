\section{Computational Chemistry Protocols and Job Specifications}

\subsection{Conformational Sampling via CREST / GFN2-xTB}
Conformational searching of flexible monomer and dimeric catalyst species was conducted using the Conformer-Rotamer Ensemble Sampling Tool (\textbf{CREST}, version 2.12) driven by the semi-empirical extended tight-binding Hamiltonian \textbf{GFN2-xTB}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sampling Method:} Metadynamics-directed conformational search (iMTD-sMD).
  \item \textbf{Energy Window:} $6.0\text{ kcal/mol}$ relative to the lowest-energy conformer found.
  \item \textbf{Solvation Model:} Analytical Linearized Poisson-Boltzmann (ALPB) implicit continuum model parameterized for toluene, tetrahydrofuran, and dichloromethane.
  \item \textbf{RMSD Clustering:} Structural clustering with an RMSD threshold of $0.12\text{ \AA}$ to eliminate duplicate conformers.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Density Functional Theory (DFT) Optimizations (ORCA \& Gaussian)}
Selected low-energy conformers from CREST ensembles were optimized at the DFT level using \textbf{ORCA 5.0} and validated against \textbf{Gaussian 16}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Exchange-Correlation Functionals:} Hybrid B3LYP and range-separated $\omega\text{B97X-D3}$.
  \item \textbf{Basis Sets:} def2-SVP for geometry optimizations and harmonic frequency calculations; def2-TZVP for single-point electronic energies.
  \item \textbf{Dispersion Corrections:} Grimme D4 or D3BJ empirical dispersion corrections.
  \item \textbf{Solvent Cavity:} SMD (Solvation Model based on Density) and CPCM.
  \item \textbf{Frequency Verification:} Harmonic vibrational frequencies were calculated for all stationary points. The absence of imaginary frequencies ($N_{\text{imag}} = 0$) confirms that each optimized geometry corresponds to a true local minimum on the potential energy surface.
\end{itemize}

\section{Statistical Thermodynamics of Catalyst Aggregation}

In Kagan's foundational $\text{ML}_2$ model, chiral monomers $M_R$ and $M_S$ undergo dimerization to form homochiral dimers $(R,R)$, $(S,S)$ and heterochiral dimer $(R,S)$:
\begin{align}
2 M_R &\rightleftharpoons (R,R) \quad (K_{\text{homo}}) \\
2 M_S &\rightleftharpoons (S,S) \quad (K_{\text{homo}}) \\
M_R + M_S &\rightleftharpoons (R,S) \quad (K_{\text{hetero}})
\end{align}

The rotational symmetry number for a homochiral dimer exhibiting $C_2$ point group symmetry is $\sigma_{\text{homo}} = 2$, whereas a heterochiral dimer possessing $C_i$ or $C_s$ symmetry has $\sigma_{\text{hetero}} = 1$. From molecular partition function theory:
\begin{equation}
q_{\text{rot}} = \frac{\pi^{1/2}}{\sigma} \left( \frac{8 \pi^2 I_A k_B T}{h^2} \right)^{1/2} \left( \frac{8 \pi^2 I_B k_B T}{h^2} \right)^{1/2} \left( \frac{8 \pi^2 I_C k_B T}{h^2} \right)^{1/2}
\end{equation}

Consequently, the ratio of equilibrium association constants incorporates an intrinsic statistical factor of 2:
\begin{equation}
\frac{K_{\text{hetero}}}{K_{\text{homo}}} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{homo}}}{\sigma_{\text{hetero}}} \exp\left( -\frac{\Delta G}{RT} \right) = 2 \exp\left( -\frac{\Delta G}{RT} \right)
\end{equation}
where $\Delta G = G_{(R,S)} - G_{(R,R)}$. Under purely statistical conditions ($\Delta G = -RT \ln 2 \approx -0.41\text{ kcal/mol}$ at $298.15\text{ K}$), $K_{\text{hetero}} = 2 K_{\text{homo}}$, producing a strictly linear NLE curve.

\section{Coupled Mass Balances and Analytical Jacobian}

Total monomer equivalents for major and minor enantiomers are defined by the catalyst enantiomeric excess $ee_{\text{cat}} \in [0, 1]$:
\begin{equation}
C_R = \frac{1 + ee_{\text{cat}}}{2}, \quad C_S = \frac{1 - ee_{\text{cat}}}{2}
\end{equation}

Conservation of mass requires:
\begin{align}
f_1(M_R, M_S) &= M_R + 2 K_{\text{homo}} M_R^2 + K_{\text{hetero}} M_R M_S - C_R = 0 \\
f_2(M_R, M_S) &= M_S + 2 K_{\text{homo}} M_S^2 + K_{\text{hetero}} M_R M_S - C_S = 0
\end{align}

To ensure quadratic convergence without numerical divergence at large binding constants ($K_{\text{homo}} \ge 10^7$), NLE-Predictor evaluates the exact analytical Jacobian matrix $J$:
\begin{equation}
J = \begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{\partial f_1}{\partial M_R} & \dfrac{\partial f_1}{\partial M_S} \\[10pt]
\dfrac{\partial f_2}{\partial M_R} & \dfrac{\partial f_2}{\partial M_S}
\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}
1 + 4 K_{\text{homo}} M_R + K_{\text{hetero}} M_S & K_{\text{hetero}} M_R \\[8pt]
K_{\text{hetero}} M_S & 1 + 4 K_{\text{homo}} M_S + K_{\text{hetero}} M_R
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}

The non-linear system is solved via Powell's hybrid method with absolute residual tolerance $< 1.0 \times 10^{-6}$.

\section{Active Dimer Kinetic Rate Equations}

When dimeric complexes are catalytically competent, product formation occurs via parallel monomeric and dimeric pathways with relative turnover numbers $g = k_D / k_M$ and $g' = k_H / k_M$:
\begin{align}
\text{Rate}_R &= k_M M_R + 2 k_D [(R,R)] \left( \frac{1 + ee_D}{2} \right) + 2 k_D [(S,S)] \left( \frac{1 - ee_D}{2} \right) + k_H [(R,S)] \\
\text{Rate}_S &= k_M M_S + 2 k_D [(R,R)] \left( \frac{1 - ee_D}{2} \right) + 2 k_D [(S,S)] \left( \frac{1 + ee_D}{2} \right) + k_H [(R,S)]
\end{align}

Evaluating the instantaneous rate ratio $\frac{\text{Rate}_R - \text{Rate}_S}{\text{Rate}_R + \text{Rate}_S}$ yields the general product enantioselectivity:
\begin{equation}
ee_{\text{prod}} = \frac{(M_R - M_S) \left[ ee_0 + 2 g \cdot ee_D \cdot K_{\text{homo}} (M_R + M_S) \right]}{(M_R + M_S) + 2 g K_{\text{homo}} (M_R^2 + M_S^2) + 2 g' K_{\text{hetero}} M_R M_S}
\end{equation}

\section{Solvent Properties and Standard-State Concentration Shift Database}

Dimerization reactions in solution ($2 M \to D$, $\Delta n = -1$) require a standard-state correction from the 1 atm ideal gas standard state to the 1 M solution standard state:
\begin{equation}
\Delta G^\circ_{\text{corr}} = -\Delta n \cdot RT \ln(V_m) = +RT \ln\left( \frac{RT}{P^\circ} \right) = +RT \ln(24.4654) \approx +1.894\text{ kcal/mol at } 298.15\text{ K}
\end{equation}
