\section{Computational Chemistry Protocols and Job Specifications}

\subsection{Conformational Sampling via CREST / GFN2-xTB}
Conformational searching of flexible monomer and dimeric catalyst species was conducted using the Conformer-Rotamer Ensemble Sampling Tool (\textbf{CREST}, version 2.12) driven by the semi-empirical extended tight-binding Hamiltonian \textbf{GFN2-xTB}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sampling Method:} Metadynamics-directed conformational search (iMTD-sMD).
  \item \textbf{Energy Window:} $6.0\text{ kcal/mol}$ relative to the lowest-energy conformer found.
  \item \textbf{Solvation Model:} Analytical Linearized Poisson-Boltzmann (ALPB) implicit continuum model parameterized for toluene, tetrahydrofuran, and dichloromethane.
  \item \textbf{RMSD Clustering:} Structural clustering with an RMSD threshold of $0.12\text{ \AA}$ to eliminate duplicate conformers.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Density Functional Theory (DFT) Optimizations (ORCA \& Gaussian)}
Selected low-energy conformers from CREST ensembles were optimized at the DFT level using \textbf{ORCA 5.0} and validated against \textbf{Gaussian 16}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Exchange-Correlation Functionals:} Hybrid B3LYP and range-separated $\omega\text{B97X-D3}$.
  \item \textbf{Basis Sets:} def2-SVP for geometry optimizations and harmonic frequency calculations; def2-TZVP for single-point electronic energies.
  \item \textbf{Dispersion Corrections:} Grimme D4 or D3BJ empirical dispersion corrections.
  \item \textbf{Solvent Cavity:} SMD (Solvation Model based on Density) and CPCM.
  \item \textbf{Frequency Verification:} Harmonic vibrational frequencies were calculated for all stationary points. The absence of imaginary frequencies ($N_{\text{imag}} = 0$) confirms that each optimized geometry corresponds to a true local minimum on the potential energy surface.
\end{itemize}

\section{Statistical Thermodynamics of Catalyst Aggregation}

In Kagan's foundational $\text{ML}_2$ model, chiral monomers $M_R$ and $M_S$ undergo dimerization to form homochiral dimers $(R,R)$, $(S,S)$ and heterochiral dimer $(R,S)$:
\begin{align}
2 M_R &\rightleftharpoons (R,R) \quad (K_{\text{homo}}) \\
2 M_S &\rightleftharpoons (S,S) \quad (K_{\text{homo}}) \\
M_R + M_S &\rightleftharpoons (R,S) \quad (K_{\text{hetero}})
\end{align}

The rotational symmetry number for a homochiral dimer exhibiting $C_2$ point group symmetry is $\sigma_{\text{homo}} = 2$, whereas a heterochiral dimer possessing $C_i$ or $C_s$ symmetry has $\sigma_{\text{hetero}} = 1$. From molecular partition function theory:
\begin{equation}
q_{\text{rot}} = \frac{\pi^{1/2}}{\sigma} \left( \frac{8 \pi^2 I_A k_B T}{h^2} \right)^{1/2} \left( \frac{8 \pi^2 I_B k_B T}{h^2} \right)^{1/2} \left( \frac{8 \pi^2 I_C k_B T}{h^2} \right)^{1/2}
\end{equation}

Consequently, the ratio of equilibrium association constants incorporates an intrinsic statistical factor of 2:
\begin{equation}
\frac{K_{\text{hetero}}}{K_{\text{homo}}} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{homo}}}{\sigma_{\text{hetero}}} \exp\left( -\frac{\Delta G}{RT} \right) = 2 \exp\left( -\frac{\Delta G}{RT} \right)
\end{equation}
where $\Delta G = G_{(R,S)} - G_{(R,R)}$. Under purely statistical conditions ($\Delta G = -RT \ln 2 \approx -0.41\text{ kcal/mol}$ at $298.15\text{ K}$), $K_{\text{hetero}} = 2 K_{\text{homo}}$, producing a strictly linear NLE curve.

\section{Coupled Mass Balances and Analytical Jacobian}

Total monomer equivalents for major and minor enantiomers are defined by the catalyst enantiomeric excess $ee_{\text{cat}} \in [0, 1]$:
\begin{equation}
C_R = \frac{1 + ee_{\text{cat}}}{2}, \quad C_S = \frac{1 - ee_{\text{cat}}}{2}
\end{equation}

Conservation of mass requires:
\begin{align}
f_1(M_R, M_S) &= M_R + 2 K_{\text{homo}} M_R^2 + K_{\text{hetero}} M_R M_S - C_R = 0 \\
f_2(M_R, M_S) &= M_S + 2 K_{\text{homo}} M_S^2 + K_{\text{hetero}} M_R M_S - C_S = 0
\end{align}

To ensure quadratic convergence without numerical divergence at large binding constants ($K_{\text{homo}} \ge 10^7$), NLE-Predictor evaluates the exact analytical Jacobian matrix $J$:
\begin{equation}
J = \begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{\partial f_1}{\partial M_R} & \dfrac{\partial f_1}{\partial M_S} \\[10pt]
\dfrac{\partial f_2}{\partial M_R} & \dfrac{\partial f_2}{\partial M_S}
\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}
1 + 4 K_{\text{homo}} M_R + K_{\text{hetero}} M_S & K_{\text{hetero}} M_R \\[8pt]
K_{\text{hetero}} M_S & 1 + 4 K_{\text{homo}} M_S + K_{\text{hetero}} M_R
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}

The non-linear system is solved via Powell's hybrid method with absolute residual tolerance $< 1.0 \times 10^{-6}$.

\section{Active Dimer Kinetic Rate Equations}

When dimeric complexes are catalytically competent, product formation occurs via parallel monomeric and dimeric pathways with relative turnover numbers $g = k_D / k_M$ and $g' = k_H / k_M$:
\begin{align}
\text{Rate}_R &= k_M M_R + 2 k_D [(R,R)] \left( \frac{1 + ee_D}{2} \right) + 2 k_D [(S,S)] \left( \frac{1 - ee_D}{2} \right) + k_H [(R,S)] \\
\text{Rate}_S &= k_M M_S + 2 k_D [(R,R)] \left( \frac{1 - ee_D}{2} \right) + 2 k_D [(S,S)] \left( \frac{1 + ee_D}{2} \right) + k_H [(R,S)]
\end{align}

Evaluating the instantaneous rate ratio $\frac{\text{Rate}_R - \text{Rate}_S}{\text{Rate}_R + \text{Rate}_S}$ yields the general product enantioselectivity:
\begin{equation}
ee_{\text{prod}} = \frac{(M_R - M_S) \left[ ee_0 + 2 g \cdot ee_D \cdot K_{\text{homo}} (M_R + M_S) \right]}{(M_R + M_S) + 2 g K_{\text{homo}} (M_R^2 + M_S^2) + 2 g' K_{\text{hetero}} M_R M_S}
\end{equation}

\section{Higher-Order Kagan Aggregation Thermodynamics ($\text{ML}_3$ and $\text{ML}_4$)}

Catalyst self-assembly in asymmetric catalysis frequently extends beyond dimerization into trimers ($\text{ML}_3$, e.g. organocatalytic proline clusters and titanium alkoxide trimers) or tetramers ($\text{ML}_4$, e.g. cubic cubane clusters in organolithium and dialkylzinc chemistry).

\subsection{Combinatorial Speciation and Symmetry Factors}
For an $n$-mer aggregate $M_n$ formed from chiral monomers $R$ and $S$, the distinct aggregate species are represented by $R_k S_{n-k}$ for $k \in \{0, 1, \dots, n\}$. Under purely statistical mixing, the association constant incorporates the combinatorial weight:
\begin{equation}
K_{k, n-k}^{\text{stat}} = \binom{n}{k} K_{\text{homo}}
\end{equation}
Energetic chiral self-recognition is parameterized by the cooperativity factor $g = \exp(-\Delta\Delta G / RT)$:
\begin{align}
\text{Trimer } (n=3): \quad &[R_3] = K_{\text{homo}} [R]^3, \quad [R_2 S] = 3 g K_{\text{homo}} [R]^2 [S], \nonumber\\
&[R S_2] = 3 g K_{\text{homo}} [R] [S]^2, \quad [S_3] = K_{\text{homo}} [S]^3 \\[6pt]
\text{Tetramer } (n=4): \quad &[R_4] = K_{\text{homo}} [R]^4, \quad [R_3 S] = 4 g K_{\text{homo}} [R]^3 [S], \nonumber\\
&[R_2 S_2] = 6 g K_{\text{homo}} [R]^2 [S]^2, \quad [R S_3] = 4 g K_{\text{homo}} [R] [S]^3, \quad [S_4] = K_{\text{homo}} [S]^4
\end{align}

\subsection{Coupled Mass Balance and Exact Analytical Jacobian}
Total monomer conservation requires:
\begin{align}
F_1([R], [S]) &= [R] + \sum_{k=1}^n k K_{k, n-k} [R]^k [S]^{n-k} - C_R = 0 \\
F_2([R], [S]) &= [S] + \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (n-k) K_{k, n-k} [R]^k [S]^{n-k} - C_S = 0
\end{align}

The exact elements of the Jacobian matrix $J$ implemented in NLE-Predictor are:
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial [R]} &= 1 + \sum_{k=1}^n k^2 K_{k, n-k} [R]^{k-1} [S]^{n-k} \\[4pt]
\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial [S]} &= \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} k (n-k) K_{k, n-k} [R]^k [S]^{n-k-1} \\[4pt]
\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial [R]} &= \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} k (n-k) K_{k, n-k} [R]^{k-1} [S]^{n-k} \\[4pt]
\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial [S]} &= 1 + \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (n-k)^2 K_{k, n-k} [R]^k [S]^{n-k-1}
\end{align}
This analytical formulation guarantees quadratic Newton-Raphson convergence even under extreme aggregation ($K_{\text{homo}} > 10^7\text{ M}^{-(n-1)}$).

\section{Asymmetric Autocatalysis and Soai Reaction Kinetic Theory}

The Soai reaction (addition of diisopropylzinc to pyrimidine-5-carbaldehyde) exhibits spontaneous chiral symmetry breaking and hyper-amplification. NLE-Predictor models the complete Blackmond square-order kinetic mechanism with mutual inhibition:
\begin{align}
\text{Aldehyde } (A) + \text{Zn}(i\text{-Pr})_2 (B) &\xrightarrow{k_0} \frac{1}{2} P_R + \frac{1}{2} P_S \quad (\text{uncatalyzed}) \\
A + B + [P_R]_{\text{free}}^2 &\xrightarrow{k_{\text{auto}}} [P_R]_{\text{free}}^2 + P_R \quad (\text{homochiral autocatalysis}) \\
A + B + [P_S]_{\text{free}}^2 &\xrightarrow{k_{\text{auto}}} [P_S]_{\text{free}}^2 + P_S \quad (\text{homochiral autocatalysis})
\end{align}

Rapid reversible dimerization sequesters the minor enantiomer into an unreactive heterochiral dimer:
\begin{equation}
P_R + P_S \xrightleftharpoons{K_{\text{hetero}}} (P_R P_S)_{\text{inactive}}
\end{equation}
When $K_{\text{hetero}} \gg K_{\text{homo}}$, $(P_R P_S)$ quantitatively quenches the minor enantiomer, allowing the enantiomeric excess to hyper-amplify from trace seeds ($ee_{\text{seed}} \approx 0.001$) to $> 99.5\%$ ee. The system of stiff differential equations is integrated numerically using a 5th-order implicit Radau IIA collocation solver.

\section{Multi-Temperature van 't Hoff Deconvolution and Confidence Ellipses}

When NLE curves are measured across multiple reaction temperatures ($T_1, T_2, \dots, T_m$), the temperature dependence of chiral self-recognition is governed by the Gibbs-Helmholtz relation:
\begin{equation}
\Delta\Delta G^\circ(T) = \Delta\Delta H^\circ - T \Delta\Delta S^\circ
\end{equation}
where $\Delta\Delta H^\circ$ (kcal/mol) reflects differential bonding enthalpy (e.g. hydrogen bonding, $\pi$-stacking) and $\Delta\Delta S^\circ$ (cal/(mol$\cdot$K)) reflects conformational reorganization and solvent desolvation entropy.

Simultaneous global non-linear least-squares minimization extracts $(\Delta\Delta H^\circ, \Delta\Delta S^\circ)$ with parameter covariance matrix $\mathbf{\Sigma} = s^2 (\mathbf{J}^T \mathbf{J})^{-1}$. The isoinversion temperature $T_{\text{inv}}$:
\begin{equation}
T_{\text{inv}} = \frac{\Delta\Delta H^\circ}{\Delta\Delta S^\circ}
\end{equation}
identifies the critical thermodynamic threshold where chiral discrimination vanishes ($\Delta\Delta G^\circ(T_{\text{inv}}) = 0$, $g = 1$, producing a linear NLE), above which asymmetric amplification can invert sign. Joint 95\% confidence ellipses in the $(\Delta\Delta H^\circ, \Delta\Delta S^\circ)$ plane explicitly quantify enthalpy-entropy compensation.

\section{Non-Parametric Bootstrap Monte Carlo Uncertainty Quantification}

To bypass the linearity assumptions of asymptotic Fisher information standard errors, NLE-Predictor provides non-parametric residual and case bootstrap resampling ($B \ge 1000$). For each replication $b \in \{1, \dots, B\}$:
\begin{equation}
y_i^{*(b)} = \hat{y}_i + r_j^{*(b)}, \quad r_j^{*(b)} \sim \text{Uniform}(\{r_1 - \bar{r}, \dots, r_N - \bar{r}\})
\end{equation}
Re-estimation across all replications provides empirical 95\% confidence intervals, parameter bias $\mathbb{E}[\hat{\theta}^*] - \hat{\theta}$, and the full non-linear joint parameter correlation distribution.
